Please refine the current **Pulse.ai** design, but do not completely change the existing structure or functionality.

### Branding

Use the **Pulse logo** consistently across the platform.

The primary Pulse brand color is:

**#0A3A5D**

Please replace the current green tones with **#0A3A5D** and suitable lighter/darker variations of this blue.

You can **retain the gold accent**, as the blue + gold combination gives the platform a premium executive feel.

The overall palette should feel:

**Corporate, premium, mature, trustworthy and executive.**

---

### Important Design Direction

The current design feels a little **too AI-generated / futuristic / modern-tech**.

I want Pulse.ai to feel more **human-designed and corporate**, as though it was carefully designed by an experienced enterprise product design team rather than generated by AI.

Please reduce the typical AI-dashboard appearance.

In particular, avoid having **everything inside separate rounded boxes/cards**.

Right now, too many individual cards make the interface feel like a generic modern SaaS dashboard.

Explore other ways of structuring information.

For example, use:

* Open layouts
* Clear sections separated through spacing rather than containers
* Subtle divider lines
* Editorial-style KPI layouts
* Structured rows
* Tables where appropriate
* Horizontal KPI summaries
* Large typography for important figures
* Small supporting information below the figure
* Charts integrated naturally into the page
* Grouped information without surrounding every element with a border
* Strong alignment and grid structure
* More whitespace
* Carefully controlled typography hierarchy

The screen should feel like an **executive financial/business report transformed into an interactive digital platform**, rather than a collection of dashboard widgets.

---

### Business Groups

For UCC, Estithmar, Assets, Aura, PIH, Baladna and TMT, avoid creating seven identical rounded cards.

Try something more sophisticated such as:

* Executive performance table
* Horizontal business summary
* Structured rows
* Ranked performance view

The President should be able to glance across the businesses very quickly.

### Ask Pulse

Keep **Ask Pulse** prominent because AI is an important capability, but don't make the entire platform look like an AI product.

The AI should feel like an **intelligence layer within a serious business platform**.

Instead of glowing gradients, AI sparkles, futuristic effects or oversized AI graphics, keep Ask Pulse simple and elegant.

For example:

**Ask Pulse**

*Ask anything about your business.*

[ Why is Assets below forecast this month?                     → ]

Suggested questions can appear subtly underneath.

The experience should feel similar to an executive search interface rather than an AI chatbot.

### Visual Style

The desired feeling is:

**Premium Corporate × Executive Intelligence × Human-Crafted Design**

Not:

**Futuristic AI Dashboard × SaaS Template × Too Many Cards**

Use:

* Pulse Blue **#0A3A5D**
* Gold accents
* White / warm off-white backgrounds
* Dark navy typography
* Subtle grey dividers
* Minimal shadows
* Limited border radius
* High-quality typography
* Strong alignment
* Generous whitespace
* Thin separators
* Sophisticated charts

Avoid:

* Excessive gradients
* Glow effects
* Glassmorphism
* Too many pill elements
* Excessive rounded corners
* Floating cards everywhere
* Neon AI styling
* AI sparkles everywhere
* Over-designed animations
* Excessive use of blue backgrounds

Pulse.ai should look appropriate on the screen of a **President, CEO or Group Leader**.

Think more in the direction of:

**Executive Annual Report + Corporate Finance Platform + Intelligent Analytics**

rather than a typical AI SaaS dashboard.
