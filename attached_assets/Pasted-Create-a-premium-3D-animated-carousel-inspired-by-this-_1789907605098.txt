Create a premium **3D animated carousel inspired by this reference animation:**
https://veloxthemes.com/components/3d-animated-carousel

I want the **motion behavior, 3D depth, entrance sequence, spacing, perspective and continuous rotation feel** to closely match the reference. Do not create a normal horizontal slider or simple cover-flow carousel.

### Core 3D Structure

Build the cards as a true **3D circular/radial carousel**.

* Cards must be positioned around an invisible horizontal 3D ring.
* Use real perspective and depth using `perspective`, `transform-style: preserve-3d`, `rotateY()` and `translateZ()` or an equivalent proper 3D implementation.
* The cards should appear to physically travel around the user rather than simply slide left and right.
* The front card should naturally appear larger and clearer.
* Cards travelling toward the sides should progressively rotate away from the viewer.
* Rear cards should become smaller/subtler because of their Z-depth.
* Maintain convincing perspective throughout the complete rotation.
* Do not flatten the carousel into 2D.

### Cinematic Entrance Animation

The initial page-load animation is extremely important.

When the component first appears:

1. Cards should begin from a **widely spread / exploded arrangement**.
2. Give the cards noticeable spacing and depth rather than immediately placing them in their final positions.
3. Cards should smoothly fly inward toward the centre.
4. At the same time, gradually establish their correct `rotateY` angle and `translateZ` position.
5. The cards should converge into a perfectly spaced 3D carousel ring.
6. Use smooth acceleration/deceleration instead of linear motion.
7. The entrance should feel cinematic and premium — not like cards suddenly popping into position.
8. Once the cards have settled, seamlessly transition into the continuous carousel rotation.

There must be **no visible jump** between the intro animation and normal rotation.

Suggested intro duration: approximately **1.8–2.5 seconds**.

Use an elegant easing curve such as a premium cubic-bezier / spring with very little bounce.

### Continuous Rotation

After the entrance:

* Automatically rotate the entire 3D ring continuously.
* Rotation should be very slow, controlled and elegant.
* Movement must remain fluid at all times.
* Use requestAnimationFrame or GPU-accelerated transforms rather than repeatedly changing layout properties.
* Rotation should loop infinitely with no restart point or visible snap.
* Default direction: left-to-right, but make direction configurable.
* Do not pause after each card.
* This is a continuous rotating 3D object, not an autoplay slider that jumps between slides.

Target roughly **20–30 seconds for one complete revolution**, depending on card count.

### Card Behaviour

Each card should:

* Have a premium rounded rectangle shape.
* Preserve the supplied image/content aspect ratio.
* Use subtle shadows appropriate to its depth.
* Keep text/images sharp on the front-facing card.
* Avoid excessive glow.
* Avoid large borders.
* Avoid unnecessary UI decoration.
* Remain part of the 3D ring rather than individually floating.

Use approximately:

* Border radius: `18–24px`
* Card width: responsive, approximately `260–340px` desktop
* Consistent card height/aspect ratio
* Large enough carousel radius so the cards do not overlap.

### Depth

Depth is one of the most important parts of this animation.

Use a substantial `translateZ` radius so that there is clearly visible space between:

* front cards
* side cards
* rear cards

The carousel should feel like an actual **3D installation floating in space**.

Do not fake depth only with `scale()`.

Perspective should be roughly in the range:

`900px – 1400px`

and the carousel radius should be calculated dynamically based on:

* number of cards
* card width
* viewport width

### Visual Hierarchy

The card closest to the viewer should have the highest visual prominence.

As cards travel away:

* slightly reduce visual emphasis
* allow perspective to naturally reduce apparent size
* optionally reduce opacity very subtly on cards furthest behind

Do not dramatically blur or darken side cards.

The complete carousel should remain visible as one elegant 3D composition.

### Interaction

Add desktop interaction without damaging the automatic animation.

* Allow click-and-drag horizontal rotation.
* Cursor should change to grab/grabbing.
* Dragging should rotate the entire ring.
* Add natural inertia after releasing.
* Temporarily reduce/stop auto-rotation while dragging.
* Smoothly resume auto-rotation afterwards.
* Touch swipe should work on mobile.

Do not allow dragging to produce uncontrolled spinning.

### Hover

When hovering a front/visible card:

* Give it only a subtle visual lift/emphasis.
* Do not remove it from its 3D position.
* Do not interrupt the carousel geometry.
* Do not introduce excessive zooming.

The animation itself should remain the hero.

### Responsive Behaviour

Desktop:

* Show the full cinematic 3D ring.
* Maintain substantial depth.

Tablet:

* Reduce card width and carousel radius proportionally.

Mobile:

* Keep the 3D concept.
* Reduce radius and number of simultaneously prominent cards if necessary.
* Do not replace it with a completely different basic slider unless performance absolutely requires it.

### Performance

The animation needs to feel exceptionally smooth.

Prioritize:

* `transform`
* `opacity`
* `will-change: transform`
* GPU-accelerated animation
* `requestAnimationFrame`

Avoid frequently updating:

* width
* margin
* left/right
* DOM layout

Aim for **60fps**.

### Accessibility

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

For reduced-motion users:

* skip the dramatic fly-in
* significantly reduce or disable continuous rotation
* keep the carousel usable

### Configuration

Make the component reusable and expose:

* cards / images
* item count
* autoRotate
* rotationSpeed
* direction
* carouselRadius / distance
* cardWidth
* cardHeight
* cornerRadius
* introAnimation
* introDelay
* introDuration
* introStartGap
* introEndGap
* introStartSpeed
* introEndSpeed

### Important

The reference must be interpreted as a **rotating 3D ring**, not a carousel with one active card and two cards beside it.

The most important sequence is:

**scattered/spread cards → cinematic convergence → cards form a 3D ring → seamless continuous rotation**

The result should feel sophisticated, smooth, spatial and expensive.

Do not redesign the surrounding page or change my existing content, typography, colours or sections. Only introduce this carousel and its motion behaviour into the specified area.

If implementing this in React, create it as a reusable component and use CSS 3D transforms with Framer Motion only where useful for the intro/interaction. Avoid unnecessary animation libraries and keep the implementation production-quality.
