Design a premium, modern, enterprise-grade responsive web platform called **Pulse.ai**.

Pulse.ai is an **AI-powered Business Analytics and Data Intelligence Platform** for a large enterprise group. It should give senior leadership a real-time view of business performance while also allowing them to interact with enterprise data conversationally using AI.

The overall experience should feel like an **Executive Command Center**, not a traditional BI dashboard. It must be clean, highly structured, corporate, intelligent, and suitable for Presidents, CEOs, and Group Leaders.

### Business Structure

The organization contains multiple business groups:

* UCC
* Estithmar
* Assets
* Aura
* PIH
* Baladna
* TMT

The application will use **SSO authentication** and support two primary roles:

**1. President**
The President has enterprise-wide access and can see consolidated information across all business groups.

**2. Group Leader**
A Group Leader should only see the business group assigned to them and its underlying companies/business units.

After SSO login, detect the user's role automatically and provide a different dashboard experience for each role.

# 1. PRESIDENT EXPERIENCE

The President landing page should act as an **Enterprise Pulse / Executive Command Center**.

The President should immediately understand:

* How the entire organization is performing
* Which groups are performing well
* Which groups require attention
* Revenue performance
* Profitability
* Budget performance
* Forecast
* Achievement %
* MTD performance
* YTD performance
* Time elapsed vs achievement
* Variance vs budget
* Variance vs forecast
* Growth compared with previous periods
* Major risks
* Major opportunities
* AI-generated insights
* Important approvals
* Important documents or attachments

Do not overload the screen with dozens of KPI cards.

Create a strong information hierarchy.

## Executive Header

At the top, show something similar to:

**Good morning, Mr. Jasim**
*Here is the pulse of your business today.*

Include:

* Current reporting period
* MTD / QTD / YTD selector
* Date filter
* Notifications
* Profile
* Last data refresh indicator

The experience should immediately feel personalized and executive-level.

---

# 2. ENTERPRISE GLANCE

The first major section should be called something like:

**Enterprise at a Glance**

Show approximately 4–6 critical consolidated KPIs such as:

* Revenue
* EBITDA / Profit
* Budget Achievement
* Forecast
* Overall Growth
* YTD Achievement

Each KPI should include useful context rather than only a number.

Example:

**Revenue**
QAR 18.4B
↑ 8.2% YoY
94% of YTD Target

Use sophisticated but minimal micro visualizations such as:

* progress indicators
* tiny trends
* variance indicators
* sparklines
* target markers

Avoid making every KPI look like a generic card.

Below the executive KPIs, show all business groups:

UCC | Estithmar | Assets | Aura | PIH | Baladna | TMT

* Revenue

* Trend
* Business health/status

**UCC**

Achievement: 96%

# 4. AI — ASK ANYTHING

One of the most important elements of Pulse.ai should be a prominent conversational AI experience.

Place a large, elegant search/AI interaction area around the middle of the dashboard.

Example heading:

**Ask Pulse**

Subtext:

*Ask anything about your business.*

Search placeholder:

**“Why is Assets revenue below forecast this month?”**

Other examples:

* Compare Baladna revenue against budget.
* Which business requires my attention today?
* Why did UCC profitability decline?
* Show companies likely to miss their annual target.
* Compare Q3 performance across all groups.
* What changed since yesterday?
* Which businesses exceeded forecast?
* Give me a summary of TMT performance.

The AI response should not simply provide text.

Depending on the question, Pulse.ai should dynamically generate:

* KPI cards
* tables
* comparison cards
* charts
* trend graphs
* timelines
* variance analysis
* forecasts
* recommendations
* supporting documents
* citations/source references
* suggested follow-up questions

The conversational experience should feel deeply integrated into the analytics platform.

# 6. AI EXECUTIVE INSIGHTS

Include an intelligent section called something like:

**Pulse Insights**

Instead of showing raw charts, automatically explain what matters.

Example:

**Assets revenue is 6.8% below forecast**

Primary contributors:

* Hospitality occupancy ↓ 4.2%
* Retail footfall ↓ 7.1%
* Commercial leasing revenue ↓ QAR 12M

AI Recommendation:

*Current trajectory indicates approximately QAR 38M potential annual revenue exposure if the trend continues.*

[Explore Insight]

Another example:

**Baladna is outperforming its YTD target by 11.4%.**

Primary driver:
Domestic sales increased significantly during the current quarter.

The AI should focus on explaining **why something happened**, not simply displaying metrics.

# 12. RESPONSIVE MOBILE EXPERIENCE

Pulse.ai must be fully responsive.

Do not simply shrink the desktop dashboard.

On mobile:

* Prioritize the most important KPIs
* Stack sections vertically
* Allow horizontal swiping for business groups
* Keep **Ask Pulse** highly accessible
* Use bottom navigation or a compact menu where appropriate
* Make approvals easy to perform
* Keep charts readable
* Convert large tables into mobile-friendly summaries/cards

Suggested mobile navigation:

Home
Businesses
Ask Pulse
Attention
Profile

The mobile experience should feel intentionally designed for senior executives checking their business while travelling.

# 14. VISUAL DESIGN DIRECTION

The design should be:

* Enterprise-grade
* Premium
* Executive
* AI-first
* Minimal
* Highly structured
* Sophisticated
* Calm
* Data-rich without feeling crowded

Avoid:

* Generic admin-dashboard design
* Excessive cards
* Excessive rounded corners
* Huge typography
* Too many gradients
* Neon AI aesthetics
* Excessive shadows
* Too many colors
* Overloading the user with charts
* Consumer-app styling

Use:

* Strong whitespace
* Clear typographic hierarchy
* Refined spacing
* Subtle borders
* Minimal radius
* Sophisticated data visualization
* Compact executive analytics
* High-quality icons
* Clean corporate typography

Think of the experience as a combination of:

**Enterprise BI + Executive Command Center + Conversational AI.**

The main philosophy should be:

**Don't show executives more data. Show them what matters, why it matters, and what they should do next.**

# 15. PRODUCT STORY

The platform experience should communicate this story:

The President opens Pulse.ai in the morning.

Within a few seconds, he understands the overall health of the organization.

He sees which business groups are ahead of plan and which ones require attention.

Pulse.ai proactively highlights a forecast risk within Assets.

Instead of navigating through multiple dashboards, he asks:

**“Why is Assets below forecast?”**

Pulse.ai analyzes the underlying data and immediately explains the major drivers.

He then asks:

**“What happens if this trend continues until December?”**

Pulse.ai generates a forecast and identifies the likely financial impact.

A budget approval appears under **Needs Your Attention**.

He reviews the underlying numbers and approves it directly from Pulse.ai.

Before leaving, he asks:

**“Give me the 5 things I should know today.”**

Pulse.ai returns a concise executive briefing across the entire organization.

The entire experience should make Pulse.ai feel less like a dashboard and more like an **AI Business Intelligence Companion for enterprise leadership.**

Create both the **President Dashboard** and **Group Leader Dashboard**, including desktop and responsive mobile layouts, while maintaining one consistent Pulse.ai design system.
